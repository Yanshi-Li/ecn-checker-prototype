Change Details
Reason for Change :
This ECN allows Jabil to use the PCB 519123 rev B1 on the following DD MC PH12 modules:

567953 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 230V DBL
567954 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 120V DBL
567955 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 230V SGL
567956 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 120V SGL

Phase 12 motor controller is PELV, which require one additional component, a ferrite bead, to meet the EMC emission requirements.
Description of Change :
Releasing the following modules:
ADD 567953 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 230V DBL
ADD 567954 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 120V DBL
ADD 567955 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 230V SGL
ADD 567956 MOD MC DD RX24T PH12J 120V SGL

About the PCB 519123:
When compared to revision A, the revision B1 present the following differences:
1) Ferrite Bead FB101 was added to the board.
2) The square frame where the QR code is laser engraved is filled in white color.
3) C314 and C315 pitch has changed from 7.63mm to 7.5mm.
4) Pin holes of R102 R146, R147 and R118 changed from 1.1mm to 1.2mm.
5) A gas vent was added below C126 and C117.
6) Silkscreen was added to faston connector P102.
7) Silkscreen was added to jumpers J101 and J103.
